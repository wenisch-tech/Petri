Compatibility

OpenAI and LiteLLM use the Responses API. Ollama and generic OpenAI-compatible servers use Chat Completions with reconstructed conversation history, which works with stateless local endpoints. Unsupported reasoning or verbosity controls can be left on “Model default”.
